# Publication risk report

## Included

- Public Chaai–HPCA architecture and responsibility boundary.
- Generic workflow stages, recovery, provenance, MLIP lifecycle, and scientific formulas.
- Honest implementation maturity and safety limitations.
- Static-site build, validation, governance, and deployment automation.

## Intentionally excluded

- Site-specific deployment profiles, accounts, paths, hosts, and module stacks.
- Live scheduler jobs, daemon locations, project state, logs, and operational recovery commands.
- Unpublished materials, numerical results, collaborator data, and partner information.
- Runtime source code and training data.
- Credentials and credential-bearing configuration.

## Required human decisions before publication

- Confirm the right to publish the HPCA name, architecture, and derived descriptions.
- Confirm the Chaai Labs ownership and license statements.
- Confirm that descriptions developed using institutional resources comply with applicable policy.
- Revoke the previously exposed GitHub token.
- Review landing-page claims about training records, model alignment, and experimental domains.

## Residual limitations

Automated scans detect common credential and infrastructure patterns but cannot prove that content is free of confidential information. Scientific and institutional human review remains mandatory.
