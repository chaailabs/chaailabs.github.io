Use Cases

⚛️
Materials Science
Accelerate discovery of next-generation materials — from battery electrolytes and heterogeneous catalysts to semiconductors and structural alloys. Chaai reasons across DFT, classical MD, and ML force fields simultaneously, closing the loop from atomistic simulation to property prediction.
  • Train and benchmark ML force fields (DeepMD, MACE) via ALCHEMI workflows
  • Automate VASP convergence checks, INCAR tuning, and OUTCAR parsing
  • Compute phonon dispersions, thermal conductivity, and defect formation energies
  • Generate and analyze phase diagrams with pymatgen and ASE
  • Run temperature-dependent LAMMPS MD scans and extract transport properties

Biological Molecules
From protein folding and membrane dynamics to enzyme catalysis and nucleic acid interactions. Chaai handles the full MD pipeline — system setup, force field parameterization, long production runs, trajectory analysis, and free energy calculations across all major bio-simulation codes.
  • Build and solvate GROMACS / AMBER systems from PDB structures
  • Compute free energy differences with FEP, MBAR, and thermodynamic integration
  • Analyze protein dynamics: RMSF, RMSD, PCA, contact maps, hydrogen bonds
  • Run QM/MM simulations for enzyme active site modeling with NAMD + ORCA
  • Predict structures with AlphaFold2 and ESMFold, then validate with MD

Pharma & Drug Design
Compress the drug discovery pipeline with AI-guided virtual screening, binding affinity prediction, and lead optimization. Chaai orchestrates large docking campaigns, interprets ADMET profiles, and integrates deep learning models directly into HPC workflows — at any scale.
  • Screen millions of compounds with AutoDock Vina or Glide via SLURM array jobs
  • Compute relative and absolute binding free energies (RBFE / ABFE) with FEP+
  • Predict ADMET properties (solubility, BBB permeability, toxicity) with DeepChem
  • Generate QSAR models and interpret SAR from large compound libraries
  • Fragment-based design and scaffold hopping with RDKit + generative AI

About Chaai Labs

HPC-native AI,
built for scientists

Chaai (Computational HPC Agentic AI) is an open-source AI platform that brings large language model reasoning directly to scientific computing. Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, Chaai is trained on real simulation workflows — not generic web text.

From converged VASP calculations and MLIP force field benchmarks, to protein MD free energy calculations and drug-target docking pipelines — Chaai speaks the language of every domain. It navigates GROMACS topology errors, interprets AlphaFold structures, runs AutoDock screening workflows, and orchestrates any Slurm cluster, all in one agent.

How It Works

From raw HPC data
to an aligned AI agent

01
Collect & Synthesize
Crawls real simulation directories for VASP, LAMMPS, and DeepMD workflows. NVIDIA Nemotron generates 800+ expert synthetic trajectories.
$ python collect_simulation_workflows.py
--roots /hpc/simulations
→ 4,247 examples collected
02
Filter & Verify
NeMo Curator removes low-quality examples. Physics verifier checks VASP/LAMMPS/DeepMD outputs against known bounds and builds DPO pairs.
$ python verify_trajectories.py
→ chosen: 1,840 examples
→ rejected: 160 examples
03
Train & Deploy
QLoRA SFT on Qwen2.5-Coder-32B, then DPO alignment on an NVIDIA H100. Lightweight LoRA adapter — no full model retraining required.
$ torchrun train_qwen.py
--model Qwen2.5-Coder-32B
--lora-r 64 --epochs 3
